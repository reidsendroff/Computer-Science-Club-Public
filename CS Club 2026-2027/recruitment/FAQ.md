# Recruitment FAQ

## Do I need experience?

No. The first ten meetings teach Java from running a first program through methods, arrays, objects, collections, and simple problem solving.

## What happens at a meeting?

A short demo, a guided build, choice time to finish/remix/stretch, member demos, and a quick feedback check. At least half the meeting is coding.

## Is this only competitive programming?

No. The club begins with programming fundamentals and includes collaborative projects. Algorithm and competition challenges become optional opportunities after prerequisites.

## What if I already know Java?

Choose stretch constraints, write tests, improve design and performance, review code, or lead a small team. Challenge choices change by task; no one receives a permanent level.

## Will I be ranked?

No public rankings or permanent ability groups. Readiness checks help leaders choose what to teach and help members find the right next task.
