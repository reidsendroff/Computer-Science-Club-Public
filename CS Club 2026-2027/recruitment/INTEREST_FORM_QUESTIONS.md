# Interest Form Questions

Use a school-approved form stored outside this public repository. Confirm data-retention and access rules with the adviser (`TBD`).

## Recommended questions

1. Name (required only for follow-up; private)
2. School-approved contact method (private)
3. Grade level (optional if school policy permits)
4. “What sounds fun?” Select any: making games/tools, learning Java, team projects, puzzles/algorithms, competitions, teaching others, unsure yet
5. “What would help you participate?” Select any: device/setup help, slower examples, written steps, partner work, extra challenge, other/private response
6. “Have you coded before?” Never / a little / regularly / prefer not to say
7. “What would make the first meeting worth attending?”
8. Permission to receive club updates under school policy

## Do not ask publicly

Do not publish responses, academic grades, schedules, phone numbers, personal email, attendance history, disability/accommodation details, or individual experience labels. Summarize only non-identifying aggregate interests for planning.
