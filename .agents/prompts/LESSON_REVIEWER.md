# Lesson Reviewer Prompt

Review a proposed lesson without editing it. Read `docs/CONTENT_STANDARDS.md`, the prerequisite map, readiness gates, and `CS Club 2026-2027/operations/LESSON_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md`.

Report findings by severity with exact file/section references. Check:

- prerequisites and sequence;
- Java correctness and compilability;
- explanation blocks of 10 minutes or less and at least 50% hands-on time;
- core/support/stretch choices, recovery path, runnable finish, and exit ticket;
- accessibility for a true beginner and meaningful stretch for experienced members;
- privacy, invented dates, broken links, missing solutions, and public ranking.

End with `approve`, `approve with minor fixes`, or `revise`, followed by the smallest changes needed.
