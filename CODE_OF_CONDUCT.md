# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want every student—especially someone writing code for the first time—to feel safe asking questions, making mistakes, and contributing. Be patient, specific, and kind. Critique code and ideas, never people.

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, humiliation, unwanted personal attention, or public ranking of members by speed or ability. Do not pressure anyone to disclose grades, experience, attendance reasons, or private feedback.

## Learning together

- Explain jargon and invite questions.
- Let learners choose support, core, or stretch work without labels.
- Share credit and rotate visible roles.
- Ask before taking over someone else's keyboard.
- Treat errors as normal evidence for debugging.

Report concerns privately to the school adviser or the appropriate school channel. School policy governs escalation and consequences; the adviser and reporting route are `TBD` until officially confirmed.
