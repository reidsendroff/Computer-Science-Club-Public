# Stable Club Context

## Purpose

The club helps students become confident programmers through Java, collaborative practice, algorithms, and small projects. No prior coding experience is required.

## Lesson learned

In 2025–2026, attendance fell from about 15 members to roughly 3–4 after the program moved too quickly into binary search, BFS, and DFS. The 2026–2027 response is structural: ten shared Java-foundation meetings, short explanations, substantial coding time, flexible challenge choices, and mastery gates before algorithms.

This history is used to improve the program, not to blame prior leaders or students.

## Program promises

- Beginners leave each meeting with something that runs.
- Members learn with others, not through public ranking.
- Advanced members have meaningful stretch work and mentoring roles without pulling the group past prerequisites.
- Algorithm instruction follows a visible dependency map.
- Projects and contests are introduced as opportunities, not prerequisites for belonging.
